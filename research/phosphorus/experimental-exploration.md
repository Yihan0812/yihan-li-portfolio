## Trapping and distinguishable-exchange strategies

The mixed phenyl/para-chlorophenyl disulfide design sought distinguishable sulfur-containing products rather than regeneration of indistinguishable PhSSPh. Page 8 also proposes bulky/Lewis-pair approaches to trapping or stabilising P3 species. The exact bulky reagent, quantities and a trapped P3 product are not established. These remain strategies/attempts, not successful isolation claims.

The crucial reassessment on page 9 is that mixed disulfide may arise from background exchange, with light mentioned. Detection is therefore not a unique fingerprint of a particular transient intermediate. This limits the interpretation of the earlier rationale without making the experiment scientifically uninformative.
