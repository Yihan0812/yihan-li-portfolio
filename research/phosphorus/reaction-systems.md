## Distinguishing the bases

Et3N is a neutral tertiary amine in the sketches; its proposed N–S and P–N adducts are cationic at N. KHMDS supplies an anionic bis(trimethylsilyl)amide unit with K+ as counterion; the proposed sulfur-transfer product PhS–N(SiMe3)2 is neutral. These are different formal reaction hypotheses, not interchangeable catalyst labels or experimentally proven cycles. Base loading/solvent information from one sketch is not transferred to another system.

## Reagent notation

Ph = C6H5; ArCl = para-ClC6H4 where the notebook explicitly says para-Cl; TMS = SiMe3. “HMDS” is expanded to a bonded N(SiMe3)2 group only where the sketch makes that reading secure. Unknown potassium species, cyclic trapping symbols and ambiguous Lewis-pair partners remain unassigned.
