RESEARCH 03 / PHOSPHORUS FUNCTIONALIZATION

Computational and Experimental Investigation of Base-Mediated White Phosphorus Functionalization

Connectivity and uncertainty

All drawn P–P and P–S bonds are single. Neutral P centres have three bonds and one lone pair; I1 P2 has two bonds, two lone pairs and formal charge −1. Each neutral SPh group has a P–S and an S–C bond. Atom labels track connectivity, not computed coordinates.

Literature and evidence boundary

Horsley Downie et al., Closing the Loop: Low-Waste Phosphorus Functionalization Enabled by Simple Disulfides, ChemSusChem 18, e202401895. DOI: 10.1002/cssc.202401895 ↗. The published motif uses thiolate attack followed by reaction of a phosphorus anion with a disulfide. Scheme 2(a)’s worked example uses KHMDS and a 4-chlorophenyl disulfide; it is not an Et₃N/PhSSPh performance result.

The initiation step that supplies thiolate under the specified project conditions is left unassigned. No Et₃N–P₄ adduct or pathway-specific transition state is drawn. The labelled candidates satisfy formal valence bookkeeping; this does not establish their chemical existence or stability. Open-access source ↗, CC BY 4.0 ↗. The motif is adapted; atom-labelled graph candidates and workflow artwork are newly drawn.
