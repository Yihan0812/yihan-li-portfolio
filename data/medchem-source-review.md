# Medicinal chemistry source review

Primary personal research sources were read in full: Yihan Li’s 7-page docking report (Desktop `medchem report.pdf`, whose title matches the requested docking report), the 21-page `medchem proposal.pdf`, and Yihan Li’s 13-page `cmc lab report-3.pdf`. A similarly named report by a different author was excluded.

The proposal includes preliminary plans and informal notes. The laboratory report controls claims about completed chemistry, reaction conditions, actual meta-chloro F2, purification and characterisation. Proposed para-chloro F2 and G1 are not presented as synthesised products. HPLC area percentages are described as chromatographic measurements with residual impurities, not absolute purity. No antiparasitic assays were completed in the described work.

The docking report controls computational methods and designs. Its future-tense synthetic route and FRET assay remain explicitly proposed even where the source uses accomplished-sounding language. No molecular dynamics, completed Mpro analogue synthesis or biological validation is claimed. Model interaction annotations remain source-derived predictions. Published activity values for candidate ligands are not personal measurements.

Figures are rectangular 288 dpi crops from original PDF renderings. Structures, geometry, arrows, labels and source panel relationships are preserved. No molecules or docking poses were generated. Main docking visual selects the clear Analogue 2 3D panel of Figure 6 rather than reproducing the dense six-panel composition. The three original designed structures appear on the detail page.

`medchem-figure-inventory.json` records source, page, figure, crop, dimensions, SHA256 and intended website use for each asset. All final crop assets were visually reviewed. Template journal headers/footers and fake template publication metadata are excluded; reports are identified as academic reports. No raw reports, identifiers, other students’ work or informal proposal notes are published.

RCSB 7S3K and its associated primary study (Glaser et al., doi:10.1021/acsptsci.2c00026) were verified for background attribution. The supplied reports remain the authority for personal contributions.
