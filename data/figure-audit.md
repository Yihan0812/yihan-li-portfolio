# Research figure audit

## Decisions

- `overview-quantification.svg` and its mobile composition: removed. Replaced by an export of editable B1/B3 elements from Project, slide 9. Native geometry retained; reference concentration and sequence excerpts excluded; palette simplified. Relative readout is stated as a separate typeset equation.
- `barcode-reference.svg`: removed. Replaced by Project slide 11’s barcode design. The experimental concentration table is not included.
- `ont-readout.svg`: removed. Replaced by the editable ONT principle schematic from Project slide 23. Motor, DNA, membrane, signal and basecalling labels are retained.
- `overview-rna.svg`, its mobile composition, `rna-reaction.svg` and `rna-probe.svg`: removed. The previous generated molecular drawings are not retained as downloadable assets. A source-grounded conceptual HTML scheme specifies DNA as the substrate and leaves reporter products and turnover unverified. Probe regions are explained in text.
- `overview-solid-state.svg`, its mobile composition and `solid-state.svg`: removed. The supplied presentations do not identify the Zhejiang device, and an accurate replacement specific to that experience is not established. The entry and detail text remain without a figure.
- `pore-comparison.svg` and `biomarker-workflow.svg`: removed. These generic illustrations are unnecessary for the research descriptions.

## Source and reuse review

Project slides 9, 11 and 23 contain editable native elements, rather than imported image copies. Their export is within the user’s explicit request to reuse figures from the supplied project. Source captions identify the slides and modifications. Conceptual external references remain listed. Exporting these elements does not assign the broader group programme or experimental outcomes to Yihan Li.

Project slide 7 contains an imported sequencing image, and Viva slide 25 includes manufacturer illustrations. An explicit reuse licence for those images was not established, so they are excluded. Viva slide 26 and Project slides 20–22 informed the conceptual RNA explanation; their unpublished molecular drawings are not reproduced.

The DNA–RNA substrate behaviour was checked against NEB’s Duplex DNase documentation. The enzyme cleaves DNA in suitable duplex substrates. This does not establish exact products, reporter integrity, RNA release, recycling, amplification or multiplexing in the proposed assay.

See `figure-sources.json` for the selection bounds, export resolution and checksums. Source decks, unselected slide content and experimental datasets are outside the public repository.
