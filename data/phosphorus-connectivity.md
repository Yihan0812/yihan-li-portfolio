SPh(1a), etc., are bookkeeping labels for phenylthio groups; they do not assign sulfur isotope provenance.

All P–P and P–S bonds drawn are single.
SPh groups are neutral, two-coordinate sulfur ligands (one P–S and one S–C bond).
Neutral P centres have three bonds and one lone pair. I1 P2 has two bonds, two lone pairs and formal charge −1.
The source proposes a generic thiolate/disulfide sequence. P4-specific labelled candidates are new formal connectivity hypotheses, not published intermediates or computed minima.
Initiating thiolate generation under Et3N is not assigned. Et3N is the user-specified project additive, not the KHMDS/4-chlorophenyl demonstration in Scheme 2(a).

Symmetry: all initial P4 edges are equivalent. After N1, {13,14,23,24} is one edge class and {34} another. The two illustrated later graphs are star and chain, not symmetry duplicates.

All diagrams are connectivity drawings, not optimised geometries. Stoichiometric checks: six net disulfide additions remove six P–P bonds and produce twelve P–S bonds. C, H, P and S balance in P4 + 6 PhSSPh → 4 P(SPh)3.
